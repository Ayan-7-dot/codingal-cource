My Fitness Routine Cards

A simple weekly routine to stay active and balanced

This routine is designed to help you stay active, improve your fitness, and maintain a healthy lifestyle. Each card represents a different activity or exercise that you can incorporate into your daily schedule. Remember to listen to your body and adjust the intensity as needed.

🤸

Morning Stretch

Start the day with simple stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck.

  • Neck rolls
  • Shoulder shrugs
  • Arm circles
  • Leg swings
10 minutes
🏃

Cardio Workout

To improve stamina and energy.

  • Jumping jacks
  • Jogging
  • Skipping
20 minutes
🏋️

Strength Training

Practise exercises to build body strength.

  • Squats
  • Push-ups
  • Planks
  • Lifting weights
15 minutes
🧘

Yoga Time

Try simple yoga poses to improve balance, flexibility, and focus.

  • Downward Dog Pose
  • Warrior Pose
  • Tree Pose
15 minutes
🚶

Evening Walk

Go for a relaxing walk to stay active and refresh your mind.

25 minutes
💧

Rest and Hydrate

Drink enough water and take proper rest after completing your workout.

All day

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes exercise, rest, healthy food, and consistency.
